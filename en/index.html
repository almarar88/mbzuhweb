About Our University

About

Learn more about us

Mohamed Bin Zayed University for Humanities is one of Abu Dhabi's premier state universities offering a wide range of academic programs in the humanities, social sciences, and philosophy. We are proud to promote Islamic and Arabic culture in its modernized form in alignment with our core values which constitute respect for human rights, harmony, and moderation. We offer graduate and postgraduate degrees in Islamic Studies and Arabic Language and Literature. We help our students to become globally aware, and to have a positive impact in the world. The university's main campus is in Abu Dhabi with a satellite campus in Ajman, Al Dhafra Branch.

Our Mission

We contribute to building a sustainable humanistic knowledge future within an advanced educational and research environment that establishes human values, strengthens national identity, and develops intellectual and religious competencies capable of innovation and global leadership.

College of Islamic Studies

The College of Islamic Studies at Mohamed Bin Zayed University for Humanities (Abu Dhabi, UAE) is dedicated to achieving excellence in Islamic education while promoting the values of moderate Islam in line with the UAE's national vision. It offers a Bachelor's Program in Islamic Studies that blends deep traditional knowledge with modern curricula, humanities, and technology. The college aims to qualify graduates with strong ethical values, critical thinking skills, and readiness for the labor market and postgraduate research.

Explore the college ←

College of Social and Human Sciences

The College of Social and Human Sciences at Mohamed Bin Zayed University for Humanities strives for leadership and excellence in an educational environment built on moderation and tolerance. It offers programs including a Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy and Ethics and a Bachelor of Social Studies in Tolerance and Peace. The college equips students with high-quality knowledge and critical thinking skills, fosters national values and social responsibility, and prepares graduates for both the labor market and advanced academic research.

Explore the college ←

College of Arts & Languages

The College of Arts & Languages at Mohamed Bin Zayed University for Humanities focuses on Arabic language, linguistics, literature, and cultural studies. It develops students’ communication, analytical, and research skills while promoting cultural awareness and engagement with human heritage.

College of Graduate Studies and Research

The College of Graduate Studies and Research at Mohamed Bin Zayed University for Humanities offers advanced academic programs and promotes high-quality research in philosophy, religion, ethics, and humanities. It aims to develop critical thinking, innovation, and scholarly excellence while preparing students for academic, research, and professional careers
